# Medium-Resolution Review

Bind the explanation to its object, reader, operational purpose, delivery
context, evidence limits, and required epistemic, systemic, and categorical
perspectives.

Identify the first material instance of:

- missing parts, states, interactions, or transitions needed for prediction;
- an implicit boundary, assumption, dependency, or authority limit;
- an alternative without its relevant consequence or trade-off;
- an operational claim stronger than its evidence;
- an entity, relation, transformation, interface, preservation, or loss that is
  conflated in a way that changes action;
- an example or implementation detail that does not clarify the model;
- exhaustive inspection that should instead be routed to high resolution.

Return the relevant passage or omission, the first structural problem, the
smallest bounded repair, and remaining material uncertainty. Do not rewrite the
explanation unless separately authorized.
