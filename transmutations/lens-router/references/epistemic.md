# Epistemic Lens

## Purpose

Inspect what is known about the object, what supports that knowledge, what
remains uncertain, and what conclusions or actions the available support
licenses.

Core question: **What are we justified in believing or doing here, and why?**

## Investigation

Begin from claims, decisions, outputs, observations, tests, and other material
that actually exists. Do not impose a fixed ontology merely because the
following distinctions are available.

Ask:

- What is being asserted, and what directly supports it?
- What was observed, tested, derived, reported, assumed, or inferred?
- Does evidence establish that work happened, support a claim about the result,
  or both?
- What remains unknown or underdetermined?
- What would falsify or weaken the conclusion?
- Which conclusions depend on unestablished assumptions?
- Does evidence support knowledge only, or does a separate rule license a
  decision or action?
- Who or what may accept, reject, promote, or act on the conclusion?
- Has evidence been confused with authority, approval, or execution permission?

Use distinctions only when they change what may responsibly be concluded or
done:

- observation versus inference;
- claim versus evidence;
- execution evidence versus claim evidence;
- hypothesis versus established result;
- local evidence versus canonical authority;
- recommendation versus authorization;
- unknown versus blocker;
- confidence versus permission.

## Forbidden jumps

Do not infer:

- truth from the mere presence of evidence;
- authority from evidence;
- execution from approval to investigate;
- canonical status from a development artifact;
- causality from correlation or sequence;
- completeness from absence of recorded uncertainty;
- correctness from successful execution alone.

## Return

Return a few load-bearing findings covering:

- supported findings and their evidence basis;
- assumptions or hypotheses;
- material uncertainty;
- authority boundaries when relevant;
- open questions created by the analysis.

Use the finding records defined in `lens-packet.md`. Compactness means relevance
filtering, not premature removal of evidence or distinctions needed by a later
resolution.
