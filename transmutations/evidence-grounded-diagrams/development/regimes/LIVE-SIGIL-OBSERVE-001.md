# Regime: LIVE-SIGIL-OBSERVE-001

## Goal

Validate evidence-backed observation of saved execution outputs.

## Prompt

- Prompt: `example-prompts/sigil-observe-medium.md`

## Required Output Patterns

- `Observer pass`
- `Telemetry updated`
- `Reflection trigger state`

## Quality Bar

- Separate witnessed output from inference.

## Anti-Patterns

- Do not edit the target during observation.

## Observability

- Record observed facts separately from inference and applied changes.

## Lessons To Capture

- Observer gaps, telemetry drift, and unsupported inference.
