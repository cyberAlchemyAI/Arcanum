# Post-fix Forward Run: Needs Evidence

Use the canonical skill at `transmutations/evidence-grounded-diagrams/SKILL.md`
in create mode.

Reader question: "Does service A cause every outage in service B?"

Permitted evidence is only INC-7: services A and B were both unavailable during
one 14-minute incident window. No mechanism, direction, counterfactual,
dependency behavior, alternative-cause analysis, or complete outage population
is supplied.

Apply the admission and causal-evidence boundaries. Preserve the complete output
contract. Do not create or persist a diagram when the causal claim exceeds the
evidence.
