# Low-Resolution Review

Bind the existing explanation to its object, reader, purpose, delivery context,
evidence limits, and required epistemic and systemic perspectives.

Identify the first material instance of:

- a passage exceeding low resolution;
- an unearned concept;
- a claim stronger than its evidence;
- a precision-sensitive term used loosely;
- a component catalogue where a relation is needed;
- implementation detail introduced before conceptual need;
- branching, recurrence, boundaries, or uncertainty simplified away.

Return the relevant passage, the first structural problem, the smallest bounded
repair, and remaining material uncertainty. Do not rewrite the explanation
unless separately authorized.
