# Findings — Unified Skill Model

## Goal

Determine the current Arcanum skill-creation process, structural schema, categorization behavior, and whether the three canonical directories can be consolidated without losing required distinctions or breaking consumers.

## TL;DR

Arcanum has one documented lifecycle but not one authoritative executable creation path; it has a native runtime representation plus several distinct structural representations, validators, and behavioral contracts. This review did not establish one comprehensive authoritative skill schema, but it also did not prove that none exists. `formulae`, `transmutations`, and `arcana` are witnessed as epistemic labels and path-bearing discovery, provenance, publication, validation, and compatibility inputs—not as tier values that select differentiated execution permissions, dispatch behavior, or dependency semantics. Naive path-blind deletion is falsified. Safe consolidation remains unwitnessed, not impossible.

## Context

The repository presents all three directory families through skill projections, yet current tooling also derives category, canonical source, public paths, and some owner references from directory placement. Three explorers independently examined lifecycle, structural/authority artifacts, and category consumers; their raw reports remain under stable Agent headings in [research.md](./research.md). The synthesis below demotes partial support to `unresolved`, distinguishes documentary assertion from executable observation, and applies the canonical Arcanum meanings of **contract**, **schema**, and **canonical artifact** (`definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:18-59,77-119,340-383`).

## Current-state synthesis

### Creation process

Documentary assertion: the framework prescribes candidate capture, classification, contract authoring, validation, trial, promotion, publication, installation, observation, and maintenance (`framework/SIGIL-DEVELOPMENT-WORKFLOW.md:7-22,161-213,236-251`; [Agent 1](./research.md#agent-1--lifecycle-and-creation-process-cartography)). Authority is distributed: `sigil-development` owns lifecycle mutation and promotion readiness (`arcana/sigil-development/SKILL.md:50-59`), `invoke` is an authoring front door rather than lifecycle owner (`spells/invoke/README.md:20-26,112-129`), and transcription/decomposition are specialized intake routes (`arcana/skill-transcriptor/SKILL.md:22-32,110-119`; `arcana/skill-decomposer/SKILL.md:22-32,78-95`).

Executable observation: bootstrap and registry publication recognize package/file predicates rather than a shared lifecycle receipt (`tools/bootstrap_arcanum.sh:420-456`; `tools/build-skill-registry.py:578-621`). The current process is therefore a documented governance sequence plus separately effective commands and filesystem transitions, not one authoritative machine state path.

### Schema, contract, and representation

Under the canonical definition, a **schema** is structural shape—fields, types, enums, references, cardinality, and parse/validation rules (`definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:77-119`). A **contract** is the behavioral and authority agreement governing meaning, ownership, evidence, boundaries, and pass/block/handoff conditions (`definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:18-59`). A canonical artifact has authority only for its declared scope; generated surfaces and evidence do not inherit that authority (`definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:340-383`).

Against those definitions, the native runtime representation is a folder with `SKILL.md`, required `name` and `description` frontmatter, and a Markdown instruction body (`$CODEX_HOME/skills/.system/skill-creator/SKILL.md:58-80`; `$CODEX_HOME/skills/.system/skill-creator/scripts/quick_validate.py:17-88`). Arcanum also has distinct structural representations for governance metadata, product metadata, dependencies, registry rows, and generated provenance (`framework/ARTIFACT-METADATA-CONSTITUTION.md:40-60,104-120`; `registry/SIGIL-DEPENDENCIES.tsv:1-5`; `tools/build-skill-registry.py:114-149`; `tools/bootstrap_arcanum.sh:844-857`; [Agent 2](./research.md#agent-2--schema-and-authority-surface-cartography)).

The inspected structural validators and parsers govern different instance shapes and have incompatible scopes: native frontmatter (`$CODEX_HOME/skills/.system/skill-creator/scripts/quick_validate.py:17-88`), artifact metadata (`tools/validate-artifact-metadata.py:239-312`), dependencies (`tools/validate-sigil-dependencies.py:17-79`), registry extraction (`tools/build-skill-registry.py:114-149`), and Claude projections (`tools/validate-claude-skills.sh:73-132`). This witnesses fragmented enforcement and no cited composing validation/precedence surface within the inspected corpus. It does not, without an exhaustive schema-artifact search, prove that no authoritative schema exists.

### Categories and operational behavior

Documentary assertion: the workflow and registry describe `formulae`, `transmutations`, and `arcana` as epistemic categories (`framework/SIGIL-DEVELOPMENT-WORKFLOW.md:12,53`; `registry/SIGILS.md:64,79,117`). Executable observation: current tooling enumerates those roots for discovery, sync, dependency resolution, registry construction, generated provenance, and CI coverage (`tools/bootstrap_arcanum.sh:372-381,420-456,1300-1317`; `tools/sync-generated-skill-package.sh:122-131`; `tools/validate-sigil-dependencies.py:12-15,47-64`; `tools/build-skill-registry.py:26-31,578-598`; `.github/workflows/claude-skills.yml:3-25`; [Agent 3](./research.md#agent-3--category-consumer-falsification)).

Candidate 4 is evaluated narrowly: **a tier value selects differentiated execution permissions, dispatch behavior, or dependency semantics**. Within the inspected corpus, dispatch and dependency edges use bare capability IDs and no cited branch grants different permissions or semantics by tier (`registry/SIGIL-DEPENDENCIES.tsv:1-5`; `tools/validate-sigil-dependencies.py:68-79`; `formulae/dispatch-spec/scripts/validate-dispatch.py:170-181,956-968`). This no-witness result does not erase the witnessed path/location/provenance behavior above.

### Consolidation implication

Executable observations falsify naive path-blind deletion. Discovery and provenance read category-bearing paths (`tools/bootstrap_arcanum.sh:372-381,1300-1317`; `tools/arcanum:360-400`); active coordinators and spell runners resolve exact category-qualified capability roots (`tools/arcanum:1118-1174`; `runtime/orchestrate/scripts/native_dispatch_coordinator.py:230-242`; `spells/task-session-until-blocker/scripts/run_chain.py:23-41`; `spells/implementation-readiness/scripts/readiness_execution.py:23-39`; `spells/implementation-readiness/scripts/execution_loop.py:23-35`). These are path-compatibility obligations: moving canonical packages without changing or preserving those resolution rules can make validators, schemas, or runtime modules undiscoverable.

The high-level conclusion remains conditional. A consolidation could be compatible only if it preserves or deliberately replaces the required authority, identity, projection, publication, and path-resolution distinctions. No completed consolidation or exhaustive downstream-consumer inventory witnesses that condition. This is not a migration plan or replacement schema.
