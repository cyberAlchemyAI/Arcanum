## Source Spine

The paper states that:

- monoidal categories are categories with tensor product encoded as a bifunctor;
- multicategories generalize categories by allowing finite lists of domains;
- Hermida showed `MonCat` is equivalent to representable multicategories;
- Leinster reformulated this result but did not publish the proof;
- the paper reconstructs Leinster's proof.

Evidence: abstract and introduction, pages 1-2.
