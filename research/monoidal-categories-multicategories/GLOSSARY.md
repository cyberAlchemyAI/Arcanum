# Glossary

## Category

- Source kind: primary-source.
- Local meaning: objects, morphisms, composition, and identities satisfying associativity and identity laws.
- Arcanum reading: a typed context with legal route steps and composition receipts.
- Promotion status: analogy-only.
- Misuse warning: do not treat every Arcanum graph as a category without defining morphisms and composition.

## Monoidal Category

- Source kind: primary-source.
- Local meaning: a category with tensor product functor, unit object, associator, unitors, and coherence conditions.
- Arcanum reading: a context where separate objects or steps can be combined while preserving rearrangement receipts.
- Promotion status: analogy-only.
- Misuse warning: tensor is not generic "combine"; it has functorial and coherence obligations.

## Unbiased Monoidal Category

- Source kind: primary-source.
- Local meaning: a monoidal category variant with n-fold tensor functors for every arity and coherence isomorphisms.
- Arcanum reading: a variadic composition model where bracketing is explicit but normalized by receipts.
- Promotion status: analogy-only.
- Misuse warning: do not erase the `gamma` and `iota` data; they are the point.

## Multicategory

- Source kind: primary-source.
- Local meaning: a category-like structure whose multimorphisms have finite input lists and one output.
- Arcanum reading: a multi-input route system.
- Promotion status: analogy-only.
- Misuse warning: not every multi-agent or multi-source workflow is a multicategory.

## Representable Multicategory

- Source kind: primary-source.
- Local meaning: a multicategory where every input list has a representing object and universal factorization multimorphism.
- Arcanum reading: a multi-input workflow whose input bundles have canonical condensation receipts.
- Promotion status: analogy-only.
- Misuse warning: representability is a strong condition, not just "has many inputs."

## Universal Multimorphism

- Source kind: primary-source.
- Local meaning: a multimorphism that lets compatible multimorphisms factor uniquely through it.
- Arcanum reading: a canonical packing step that preserves all downstream interaction information.
- Promotion status: analogy-only.
- Misuse warning: uniqueness is essential.

## Leinster-Hermida Equivalence Theorem

- Source kind: primary-source.
- Local meaning: `MonCat` is equivalent to `RMulticat`.
- Arcanum reading: the binary/variadic tensor view and the representable multi-input view encode the same structured information.
- Promotion status: local-research-only.
- Misuse warning: the theorem does not equate monoidal categories with all multicategories.
