# Arcanum Bridge Decision

## Borrow Carefully

- Canonical packing: representability teaches that multi-input workflows need
  a named, receipt-bearing way to condense inputs before claiming equivalence
  to a single-surface workflow.
- Coherence receipts: monoidal coherence teaches that rearrangement freedom
  must be backed by explicit witnesses.
- Equivalence criteria: full, faithful, and essentially surjective is a useful
  diagnostic lens for "same structure, different encoding."

## Analogy Only

- `monoidal category`
- `multicategory`
- `representable multicategory`
- `universal multimorphism`
- `underlying multicategory`
- `coherence isomorphism`

These can teach Arcanum design, but they are not Arcanum canon.

## Block

- Do not call every multi-agent or multi-source workflow a multicategory.
- Do not rename Arcanum handoff bundles as representing objects without a
  uniqueness/factorization contract.
- Do not treat equivalence of categories as "same implementation."
- Do not promote tensor/coherence notation into runtime terminology.

## Promotion Candidates

None in this run. The safe output is a local operator lens, not a vocabulary
promotion.
