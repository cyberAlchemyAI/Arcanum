# Research

This folder contains Arcanum proof runs, design investigations, and framework experiments.

## Research Towers

- [AutoBayes](autobayes/) - source-first research tower for AutoBayes paper understanding, residue closure, and Arcanum bridge decisions.
- [Monoidal Categories and Multicategories](monoidal-categories-multicategories/) - Research Tower test run for a notation-heavy category theory paper reconstructing the Leinster-Hermida equivalence.
- [MOGT Agentic Conversation](mogt-agentic-conversation/) - publication research project for multi-objective game theory in agentic conversation decisions, including experiment bundles, paper contracts, and the Dispatch Spec publication DAG.

## Shared Research Aids

- [Shared Research Notation Glossary](shared-notation-glossary.md) - reusable notation reader for mathematical/formal papers. Local research towers can reference it and add paper-specific notation bridges.

## Proofs

- [Ontology Vault Branching](proofs/ontology-vault-branching/) - demonstrates business ontology, system ontology, bridge edges, traceability, and drift reporting with a neutral sample vault.
