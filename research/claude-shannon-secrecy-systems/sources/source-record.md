## Source Boundary

The paper says it develops a mathematical theory of secrecy systems and limits
the treatment to systems where the message meaning is hidden by cipher or code
over discrete symbols. Evidence: Internet Archive OCR text, lines 7-34.

The paper states that the material originally appeared in a confidential report
called "A Mathematical Theory of Cryptography," dated September 1, 1945, later
declassified. Evidence: Internet Archive OCR text, lines 40-41.

The paper explicitly cites "A Mathematical Theory of Communication" as the
source of entropy, redundancy, and related information-theoretic concepts used
in the secrecy analysis. Evidence: Internet Archive OCR text, lines 43-44 and
1410-1415.
