# Interrogation refine-design-review — composed design critique

## Q1. Can the composed design fail?

Yes, but **not uniformly**. H2 (co-location with the blind rubric) and H1's
*invariance* clause are genuinely falsifiable. The bare "a U exists" claim is **not**
falsifiable, because Proxy B's two-part code guarantees a U by construction. **Repair
(load-bearing):** pre-register that B's U is *zero-information*; all evidential weight
sits on `r*` invariance + blind-rubric co-location.

## Q2. Is the headline finding (A is monotone, not U) a problem or a feature?

Feature. It converts a vague "entropy is U-shaped" into a precise, decomposed claim: a
descending spread arm (A) plus an ascending overload arm (C) that must *cross*. It also
retro-validates the definition card's four-way split. Keep it central in the plan.

## Q3. Biggest circularity risk?

The SCU rubric. If raters see size/proxy values, co-location is manufactured. **Repair:**
freeze rubric text (hash-committed), blind raters to `r`, MDL, and bundle size, lodge
scores before reading proxy values.

## Q4. Biggest "unfalsifiable save" risk?

Proxy C's "absorbed pressure" survivorship: a flat overload arm can always be re-narrated
as hidden-but-repaired pressure. **Repair:** pre-commit a hard repair budget; un-rising C
at that budget is a falsification of the overload arm, not evidence of hidden pressure.

## Q5. Garden of forking paths?

Swapping reference models, coders, N, or dropping proxies until a U appears. **Repair:**
pre-register exact models, serializers, MDL coder, seed count, size grid, full corpus
list, and a frozen analysis script; any post-hoc change demotes the result to exploratory.

## Verdict

**flag — proceed once the five pre-registration locks are in place.** The locks are not
polish; without them the design is partially unfalsifiable. They are handed to the pilot
(stage 8) to operationalize at fixture scale before any full sweep is funded.
