## Decision Summary

AutoBayes should not be imported into Arcanum as vocabulary canon. It is most useful as a disciplined analogy and design pressure for local composition:

```text
declaration surface
  -> handoff / inverse-reading behavior
  -> local evidence or objective pressure
  -> legitimate knobs
  -> runtime interpretation
```

The safe move is to borrow structural checks and artifact habits, not mathematical terms. Arcanum can immediately borrow the *separation discipline* while keeping AutoBayes-specific objects as research residue until worked examples prove they help Arcanum governance rather than decorate it.
