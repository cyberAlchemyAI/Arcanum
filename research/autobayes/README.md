# AutoBayes Research Tower

## Thesis

This tower studies **AutoBayes: A Compositional Framework for Generalized Variational Inference** as a live Arcanum-adjacent research object.

The paper's central move is not merely "category theory for variational inference." Its deeper shape is:

```text
model syntax
  -> inversion structure
  -> local loss composition
  -> parameter exposure
  -> optimization semantics
```

For Arcanum, that reads like a familiar governance problem:

```text
sigil / spell surface
  -> handoff and inversion discipline
  -> local evidence and residue accounting
  -> exposed knobs / parameters
  -> runtime semantics and validation receipts
```

The goal is to understand the paper in the language of the person who built Arcanum: as a compositional account of how local contracts become global behavior without collapsing syntax, semantics, evidence, or optimization into one vague layer.

## Corpus Spine

Primary source:

- Smithe and Perin, **AutoBayes: A Compositional Framework for Generalized Variational Inference**, arXiv:2503.18608v2, 2025-03-25.

Related-paper lanes named by the paper:

- Bayesian inversion chain rule: Braithwaite, Hedges, and St Clere Smithe, **The Compositional Structure of Bayesian Inference**.
- Generalized variational inference: Knoblauch, Jewson, and Damoulas, **Generalized Variational Inference**.
- Bayesian learning rule: Khan and Rue, **The Bayesian Learning Rule**.
- Compositional statistical games: St Clere Smithe, **Approximate Inference via Fibrations of Statistical Games**.
- Probabilistic-program semantics: Staton, **Commutative Semantics for Probabilistic Programming**.
- Categorical Bayesian networks: Fong, **Causal Theories**.
- Probabilistic programming / guide programs: Pyro, deep amortized inference, and programmable MCMC with soundly composed guide programs.
- Compositional game theory: Ghani, Hedges, Winschel, and Zahn.

## Operating Rule

Every investigation must end with one mark:

- `closed-source`: directly grounded in the paper or a cited related paper.
- `closed-definition`: stabilized as an operator-facing definition for this research tower.
- `closed-distill`: reduced to a reusable Arcanum mental model.
- `closed-negative`: shown not to map cleanly into Arcanum.
- `promoted-residue`: sharpened into a next research object.

No result in this tower promotes canonical Arcanum glossary, ontology, sigil, spell, or runtime authority by itself.
