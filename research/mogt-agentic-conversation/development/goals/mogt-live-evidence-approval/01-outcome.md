# Outcome

Execute `MOGT-LIVE-EVIDENCE-APPROVAL` as an approval-gate task.

Produce a live-evidence approval decision for whether MOGT can proceed to
claim-bearing live or approved evidence runs for E1, E2, and E4.

E3 is second-wave by default and must not be included in first-wave live
approval unless the task records an explicit override and the required E3 repair
evidence.

Completion evidence must include:

- approval decision artifact;
- calibration-set requirement check;
- E1/E2/E4 protocol gate check;
- E3 second-wave/default decision;
- evidence mutation policy check;
- next goal command or blocked reason.
