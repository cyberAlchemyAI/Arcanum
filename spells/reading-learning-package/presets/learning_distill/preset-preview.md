# Preset Preview — `learning_distill` (Veritasium)

A reusable Whisper / reading-learning-package preset for **distilling a tool down
to a first mental model** for people who do not yet know the tool *or* much about
AI. The goal is not coverage — it is the click of understanding.

## Voice

- **Persona:** Feynman, Richard (from the subagents agent pool) — plain-spoken,
  analogy-first, allergic to jargon for its own sake.
- **Tone:** curious. Start from a real, slightly surprising problem.
- **Forbidden:** jargon gatekeeping, fake hype, talking down, overclaiming.

## Reader

- A newcomer, intimidated, with no shared vocabulary yet.
- Reward they leave with: a working mental model **plus** a recipe they can run today.

## Movement

1. The surprising problem (the hook).
2. One analogy — carried end to end.
3. How it actually works (the real mechanism, briefly).
4. A 5-minute recipe they can run.
5. Going further (the controller agent).
6. Source sheet (where every claim came from).

## Output shape

- 900–1800 words, compact, one running analogy.
- Print-ready HTML + downloadable PDF.
- Honest limits stated, not hidden.

## When to pick this preset

Pick `learning_distill` over `medium_explanation` when the reader has **no**
background and the win condition is "they get it and try it," not "they can cite
the evidence." Pick `quick_video` instead when you need a 60–180s script rather
than a runnable recipe.
