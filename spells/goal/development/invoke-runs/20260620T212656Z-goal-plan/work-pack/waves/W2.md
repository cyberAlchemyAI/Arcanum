# Wave W2: Delegation And Staging

## Layer Mapping

- Layer: L2
- Layer question: After this layer, we know whether eligible nodes can route
  through owners, join terminal receipts, audit evidence, and stage deltas
  without applying them.
- Promotion evidence: dispatch route validation, terminal receipt, audit
  verdict, staged delta shape check.

## Included Tasks

| Task | SWUs | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TASK-GOAL-DELEGATION-STAGING | SWU-GOAL-005, SWU-GOAL-006 | blocked-after-W1 |

## Entry Gate

- W1 read-only skeleton exits pass.
- Owner map and route validation surface are available.
- Receipt and staged-delta schemas remain consistent with contracts.

## Exit Evidence

- Eligible node route names owner, technique, receipt, gate, and fallback.
- Delegated lane has terminal receipt.
- Audit veto can block apparent success.
- Source-changing progress becomes staged delta only.

## Parallelization

Limited. Route/receipt implementation and audit/staging implementation may
prepare separately only when their write scopes are disjoint and merge through
one staged-delta contract.

## Stop Conditions

- Route validation fails.
- Delegated lane remains open.
- Audit evidence is weaker than done criteria.
- Active ledger mutation is attempted.
