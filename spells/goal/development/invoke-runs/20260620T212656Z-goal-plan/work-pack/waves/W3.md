- Staged deltas cannot bypass approval.

## Exit Evidence

- Approval token binds exact batch, approval source, state, and durable decision
  record.
- Ambient approval is rejected.
- Gap discovery terminates by budget and dedupe rules.
- Experiment Harness proves low, medium, and protected-mutation scenarios.
- Runtime installer dry-run or approved apply generates surfaces without
  hand-authored generated files.

## Parallelization

Fixture outlines may be prepared early, but Experiment Harness pass evidence
depends on implemented W1-W3 runtime behavior. Generated package work waits for
experiment evidence unless Spellcraft chooses a dry-run-only route.

## Stop Conditions

- Approval token is missing or ambient.
- Gap discovery reopens active frontier directly.
- Runtime evidence is used as reusable readiness evidence.
- Generated surfaces are hand-authored.
