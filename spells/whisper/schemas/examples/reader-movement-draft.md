# Reader Movement Contract Fixture

A thermostat begins with a familiar pressure: the room has drifted from the temperature someone chose.

Its sensor turns that difference into a signal, so the controller can decide whether heating is needed.

That relation explains why the heater may stop before the room feels warm: the system responds to the measured boundary, not to a promise of comfort.

The example can now carry a name, but the validator still cannot decide whether this explanation changed what a reader understands.
