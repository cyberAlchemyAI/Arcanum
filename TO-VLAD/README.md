# TO-VLAD — memo series

External review memos to **Vlad** (Arcanum maintainer, `cyberAlchemyAI/Arcanum`) from **Victor**, each synthesized from a multi-agent audit of the repo (deep slice-audit → skeptics/devil's-advocate → reviser). They are *drafts for discussion*, not change orders.

The series has two halves. **Memos 1–5 are gap-audits** — each points an audit at one Arcanum object and reports what is missing. **Memos 6–8 shift kind: they hand over converged *designs*** for governing multi-agent subagent dispatches, and have since been folded into a single current reading — [`DISPATCH-COMPOSITION-MODEL.md`](DISPATCH-COMPOSITION-MODEL.md) — which **supersedes** them; 6, 7, and 8 stay frozen as its provenance.

## Shared method & discipline

- **Audit pipeline:** several agents audit disjoint slices; separate agents challenge the findings; a reviser keeps only what survives the challenge. Most proposals get cut on the way (memo 2 cut 8 of 10 edits and 4 of 10 kinds).
- **Subset rule (claim ≤ proof):** every finding must stand on plain engineering merit. Where categorical / framework vocabulary appears, it is kept only when it predicts something the engineering-only reading would miss; otherwise it is stripped (both memos have a "what I deliberately did not recommend" section recording the cuts).
- **Cite, don't rediscover:** prior art (e.g. `domainspec/TAXONOMY.md`) is credited as parent pattern in the body, not as a footnote.

## How they relate

Layered, not parallel. Memo 1 names what is missing *at* the sigil layer (residue, source-schema, contract-join, measured tower). Memo 2 names the layer *underneath*: the type system that sigils and spells are nodes of. Memo 3 names the layer *on top*: how a typed, scored sigil improves over time. The dependency runs one way — **typing → scoring → optimization** — so read in order: **TO-VLAD.md → TO-VLAD2.md → TO-VLAD3.md**. None is independent corroboration of the others; all three share one audit pipeline, one observation seen from three depths.

Memo 4 steps *off* the sigil object entirely: it audits Craft, a candidate *method primitive* that composes sigils. It is not another layer of the same stack — it is the same residue-typing discipline turned on a method-level artifact, and it finds the memo-1 defect (untyped residue) reproduced one altitude up. Read it last; it presupposes memo 1's notion of typed residue to make its load-bearing finding.

Memo 5 steps up once more: its object is neither a sigil nor a method but the *framework's own governance constitution* — it asks whether Arcanum publishes the claim-vs-evidence discipline that all five memos run on. The recursion rhymes with memo 4 (auditor's discipline > audited object's), one altitude higher. It is the most deflationary of the five: a three-part gap collapsed under the skeptic pass to one load-bearing finding (promotion is gated, novelty is not), the other two reduced to a rename and a deferral.

**Memos 6–8 are not a sixth depth of the same audit — they change kind.** Where 1–5 point at an object and report a gap, 6–8 hand over a *design* for one object the whole series circles: the multi-agent subagent dispatch. They build on each other linearly. Memo 6 lays the **per-Wave composition model** (typed edges, the bounded `reopens` cycle, `pass | flag | block`, lanes-vs-roles, three observability levels) in Craft's vocabulary. Memo 7 is a lateral move — a **placement argument** for `x-ray` — and contributes only one thing back to the dispatch line: the observation that x-ray and the discovery machinery are the *same split-and-compose shape*, which is why x-ray composes downstream rather than competing. Memo 8 lifts memo 6 to the **methodology** altitude: it splits Wave from Layer (the four-level ontology), names strategy-as-typed-object, lands the mode-conflation finding, and crosswalks the whole thing onto Arcanum's `dispatch-spec` — ending in the cross-repo ownership decision only Vlad can make.

`DISPATCH-COMPOSITION-MODEL.md` **closes the line by consolidating it**: 8's four-level ontology wins over 6's coarser per-Wave collapse where they diverge, 6 keeps the typed-edge / verdict / observability / lanes-vs-roles vocabulary, and 7 enters only through its split-and-compose slice. So the reading order for the dispatch work is **the consolidated model first**; 6 → 7 → 8 are provenance you open only to see *why* a claim survived or died. The dependency in this half runs **composition (6) → placement (7) → methodology (8) → consolidation**, the mirror of the typing→scoring→optimization dependency that ordered 1–3.

**Axes (full series):** memos 1–2 are the *vertical / static* axis (what sigils **are**, **how they relate**); memo 3 the *horizontal / dynamic* axis (how a sigil **improves**); memo 4 the *method* axis (whether a sigil-composing method types its own unit); memo 5 the *meta-governance* axis (whether the framework's constitution encodes its audits' discipline); and memos 6–8 the *dispatch-composition* axis (how a multi-agent dispatch is composed, typed, and observed) — the only half that ends in a converged design rather than a gap report.
