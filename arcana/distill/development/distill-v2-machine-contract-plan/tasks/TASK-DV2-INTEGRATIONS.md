Behavior: prove separately versioned projections of the accepted semantic family.

This SWU is cross-owner. Before execution, the Invoke owner must accept exact
paths, baselines, schema versions, compatibility policy, consumers, and tests.

Acceptance requires:

- historical Invoke adapters remain readable;
- new writers project strict selected v2 vocabulary only;
- direct and invoked paths bind the same semantic result family;
- runtime events and telemetry are derived non-authoritative projections;
- evidence cannot alter substantive verdict, selected unit, or next route;
- the known stale historical `WORK-PACK.md` ref remains a named red fixture or is
  repaired under its separate owner—not silently refreshed here.
