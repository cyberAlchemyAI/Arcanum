# TASK-DV2-DECISIONS — Freeze The Semantic Contract Graph

## Objective

Replace the stale five-schema option surface with one immutable owner decision
over the eight schemas and ten semantic choices in `../SCHEMA-PLAN.md`.

## Nature Of Work

This is a human decision gate, not an implementation SWU. Planning may prepare
an amended request and independent review, but no agent may infer the selection.

## Required Decision Evidence

- exact candidate paths and `$id` values;
- strict or compatibility writer baseline;
- exact mode budgets/maxima and role programs;
- canonical technique IDs, hooks, activation, skip, and failure semantics;
- raw-intent/normalized-source boundary and exact profile binding law;
- trace ordering/supersession, verdict/route conditionals, evidence-family rule;
- finalizer authority ceiling;
- one digest over the complete immutable decision graph and one independent review.

## Exit

An exact owner selection may unlock SWU-DV2-001. Review, rehearsal, or option
admissibility alone does not. Any semantic change after selection creates a new graph.

## Forbidden Effects

No canonical schema, instance, producer, adapter, generated mirror, Git,
publication, deployment, or external-effect mutation.
