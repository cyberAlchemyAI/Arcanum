# EvidenceSet Comparison

## Purpose

Compare the first EvidenceSet candidate with the Craft stressor candidate.

## Candidates

| Candidate | Included Cards | Excluded Cards | Main Pressure | Verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| `evidence-set.evidenceset-need` | 4 | 2 | Boundary, retrieval, exclusion, open schema question. | Useful but light. |
| `evidence-set.craft-recursive-ledger` | 7 | 2 | Nested contexts, artifacts, relationships, gates, blockers, enablers, recomposition. | Stronger signal. |

## Findings

- The first candidate proved that retrieval can naturally produce a grouped object.
- The Craft candidate proves that the grouped object can preserve relationship context that is awkward to reconstruct from independent cards.
- Craft also shows that an EvidenceSet needs boundaries: it should not become a ledger, schema, synthesis essay, or Context Builder pack.

## Recommendation

Promote `EvidenceSet` only to the next design step, not directly to canonical production behavior.

Next step: define a minimal EvidenceSet schema/task with strict non-goals and validate it against both candidates.
