## Signal Summary

The existing L0-L6 taxonomy, hard/soft/human separation, source-card model, and
fixture-first promotion rule are sound. The material gap is not another scoring
model or visual-quality authority. It is richer planning-to-scenario
translation, expectation provenance, and stress/adaptation fixture coverage.

The sigil remains a seed overall. A deterministic terminal-outcome kernel has
passing development evidence, while executable Playwright browser checks and
calibrated fixture evidence remain pending.

## Patterns Found

1. Current scenario inputs emphasize routes, viewports, tasks, and source-card
   overlays but do not explicitly model design authority, realistic content
   ranges, input contexts, faults, recovery, or persistence.
2. Observed incumbent values can be mistaken for normative design contracts
   unless provenance is explicit.
3. External expert methods can efficiently generate scenario candidates, but
   their opinions and numeric heuristics do not justify hard gates.
4. Visual critique is vulnerable to anchoring when automated findings are
   revealed first; independent-pass provenance improves review quality without
   changing evidence authority.
5. The existing dense-expert false-positive fixture should be complemented by
   an aesthetically unconventional but deterministic-contract-complete trap.

## Proposed Changes

- Add optional surface, design-authority, content, context, state, and fault
  planning inputs.
- Add a revision-pinned external-source card contract with allowed-use and
  hard-gate-ceiling fields.
- Compile declared planning profiles into scenarios without inventing product
  requirements.
- Require `expectation_authority` on asserted outcomes and findings.
- Expand the fixture plan for localization, RTL, zoom, reduced motion,
  pointer/hover, offline/timeout, permission, double submission, persistence,
  theme/contrast, and aesthetic false-positive coverage.
- Separate unanchored screenshot review from deterministic evidence collection.
- Preserve the existing evidence classes and fixture-first promotion boundary.

## Explicitly Rejected

- Impeccable package, hooks, detector, Puppeteer runtime, overlay, root-file
  protocol, visual system, or copied implementation.
- Aggregate design or usability scores.
- Universal aesthetic bans or fixed unsourced thresholds.
- Detector findings as evidence authority.
- Synthetic personas as proof of comprehension, trust, task success, or
  emotional fit.
- External methods as the sole authority for any L0-L3 hard gate.

## Thresholds And Next Trigger

No reflection threshold changes are proposed. Run the next reflection after
either:

- the expanded fixture corpus and scenario parser produce an Experiment Harness
  report;
- three related expectation-authority or scenario-compilation gaps are
  observed; or
- one severe gap promotes an external-method-only claim into a hard gate.

## Iteration Decision
