## Final synthesis

**`ux-lessons` should be a *thin sigil*, not a net-new full sigil and not a schema-less spell.** It owns exactly two typed artifacts — **`lesson`** (one contextual iteration unit) and **`ux-pattern`** (the reusable distillate) — plus **two consumer adapters**, and **composes** five existing owners for everything else (signal-observer/observed-invocation-loop for session signal, workflow-reflect's analysis *shape*, distill for reduction, architecture-pattern-inventory's card store under a `ux` tag, residuality-spec for residue).

This resolves the central tension surfaced by the tensioned pair: build-from-owned (no duplicate engines or stores) **with** a real artifact contract (which a spell can't own).

The pipeline `session → lesson → ux-pattern → { validator intake, studio intake }` was **falsified-tested against this very x-ray session** (toy_game) and **survived**: three real iteration moves became lessons; the move to a co-visible right-rail became the worked pattern **`detail-beside-the-subject`**; it emitted a ux-evidence-validator claim map AND a ui-prototyping-studio `CommentEvent`→`MutationTask` with **zero invented fields**. The one honest limit — anecdote-signal patterns cannot drive a validator *hard gate* until cross-session signal accrues — is the schema working, not a defect.

**Consumer readiness:** the **ux-evidence-validator** adapter and the **ui-prototyping-studio annotation** adapter are ready to specify now. The studio **variant/fitness** intake is deferred behind a named unblock (studio OQ-5 + an axe/layout fitness evaluator).

**Honesty:** ships as a **seed**; claim ≤ proof. Promotion needs experiment-harness evidence across more than one session.
