A skill file is not one instruction — it's a stack of control layers. Each layer uses a different linguistic device (a "nudge") operating at a different altitude of abstraction: a binding harness contract at the floor, an invariant skeleton above it, discretionary branching higher still, hard stop-gates, proof obligations, and finally the output shape. Toggle, isolate, or trace a reasoning moment to see which layers fire and in what order. Then switch to Calls · loop for the other dimension — what refine does: the 10-stage canonical loop where it delegates to invoke (define · design · plan), interrogation, distill, and context-builder.
