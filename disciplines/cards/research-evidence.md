# Research Evidence Discipline

Status: active-pattern
Steward: Research Evidence Harness and research tower

## Purpose

Govern research-facing evidence so proof, hypothesis, conflict, protocol, result, and publication claims remain separated and reviewable.

## Boundary

This discipline names research evidence practice. It does not prove claims by catalog entry, publish results, or replace the research package's protocol and adjudication lifecycle.

## Evidence

- [Research Evidence Harness](../../arcana/research-evidence-harness/README.md) - defines validated run schemas, metrics, result summaries, and claim adjudication readiness.
- [Discipline Catalog](../DISCIPLINES.md) - records `research-evidence` as an active-pattern discipline.

## Validation

- Mode: mixed
- Check: research harness validation, claim-adjudication review, and `python3 disciplines/scripts/validate-discipline-catalog.py`.
- Latest result: pass

## Quality Bar

A useful research evidence discipline entry must:

- separate proof, hypothesis, conflict, protocol, result, and publication evidence,
- cite research-owned validation surfaces,
- preserve source-first claims,
- name adjudication readiness and residual uncertainty,
- keep publication or proof authority with the research owner.

## Promotion Guardrail

Research evidence can support claims under its research lifecycle, but it cannot directly promote ontology, glossary, registry, sigil, spell, or discipline authority.
