# Distillation Discipline

Status: active-pattern
Steward: Distill

## Purpose

Govern reduction of large concepts into the smallest coherent unit that can be recomposed without losing the load-bearing idea.

## Boundary

This discipline names distillation practice. It does not replace source evidence, make downstream design decisions, or promote reduced concepts into canonical definitions.

## Evidence

- [Distill](../../arcana/distill/README.md) - defines concept reduction and recomposition support.
- [Hidden Discipline Scan](../development/HIDDEN-DISCIPLINE-SCAN.md) - identifies distillation as a recurring practice.
- [Discipline Catalog](../DISCIPLINES.md) - records `distillation` as an active-pattern discipline.

## Quality Bar

A useful distillation discipline entry must:

- preserve the smallest coherent unit,
- keep source evidence attached or recoverable,
- distinguish reduced concepts from canonical definitions,
- avoid oversized discipline definitions,
- name the recomposition context or owner.

## Promotion Guardrail

Distillation evidence can clarify a route or artifact, but it cannot directly promote glossary, ontology, registry, sigil, spell, or discipline knowledge.
