# Craft Discipline

Status: candidate
Steward: Craft development package

## Purpose

Govern intention-to-artifact work that uses a recursive ledger, local evidence, validation, and promotion deferral to keep evolving work traceable without turning every local move into framework authority.

## Boundary

This discipline names the recurring Craft practice. It does not own capability promotion, task execution, registry changes, or the content of any project-local Craft ledger. Execution routes through Craft, task-session, and the repository owner that owns the target ledger.

## Quality Bar

A useful Craft discipline entry must:

- distinguish Craft method evidence from canonical framework authority,
- cite local Craft artifacts rather than abstract process language,
- keep project-local ledgers owned by their repositories,
- require receipt-backed validation before promotion,
- name the next hardening move without widening Craft into unrelated lifecycle work.

## Promotion Guardrail

Discipline evidence can recommend a route, but it cannot directly promote registry, ontology, glossary, sigil, or spell knowledge. Craft remains `candidate` until repeated local use and receipt-backed validation justify a governed status change.
