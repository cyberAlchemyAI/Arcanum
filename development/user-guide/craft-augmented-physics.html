Toward an augmented physics of making.

Craft studies how intention becomes a stable artifact through chosen structure, attention-bounded translation, local validation, residue, reflection, and recomposition.

An evolving research horizon for understanding how intent becomes form. Operational claims remain local, inspectable, and governed.

Vision 01 · System Atlas

The complete operational model.

The atlas follows one craft operation from intent to artifact, then classifies the residue and decides whether to close, repair, route, or open a new layer.

01 · Core operation

Intent becomes artifact through a governed relation.

  • Intent names the desired change.
  • Schema is the chosen representation of structure, constraints, and method.
  • Translator combines human intention, AI behavior, context, tools, vocabulary, validation, and feedback.
  • Data / Artifact is the produced, observed, or executed instance.
  • Validation compares artifact back to schema under closure criteria.
  • Residue is meaningful mismatch, loss, ambiguity, drift, or unexpressed structure.

04 · Lifecycle and plan

The outer cycle and local SWU loop are different scales.

Define Design Plan Execute Validate Reflect

The lifecycle governs the current objective or craft layer.

PlanWavesTasksSWUs

An SWU is an executable SCU. The plan closes when the next SWU has clear inputs, outputs, completion criteria, validation, failure behavior, and recomposition.

selected SWU: execute → validate locally → recompose

The local sequence does not restart the complete lifecycle. Evidence reopens only the responsible earlier phase.

06 · Governance, reflection, stop

Escalate only when local repair cannot close the residue.

  • Constitutions govern required form, lifecycle boundaries, sources of truth, gates, and composition.
  • Axioms govern load-bearing behavior that must remain invariant.
  • Reflection Tower creates a new schema/data layer only when local repair cannot close.
  • Stop when residue is acceptable, another owner must decide, or more splitting removes meaning.
Horizon

Operational claims stay local and validated. The wider research program asks whether making across domains shares a recursive pattern: difference → intention → schema → translation → manifestation → residue → reflection → recomposition.

Vision 02 · Layered SCU Map

Coherence changes with abstraction level.

SCU is not one fixed object type. It is a layer-relative test for the smallest unit that keeps enough meaning to translate, validate, and return to its upper context.

The coherence lens

Smallest coherent unit, not smallest possible fragment.

Too small loses semantic relations and creates recomposition ambiguity. Too large mixes responsibilities and increases attention loss, relation drift, and residue.

Research horizon

A disciplined question about making.

Craft can pursue a broad philosophy only by preserving the local evidence boundary that makes each operation inspectable.

Honesty boundary

Every local use must still answer:

  1. What was the chosen schema?
  2. What data or artifact did it produce?
  3. What validation compared artifact back to schema?
  4. What residue remained?
  5. What repair, route, new layer, or stop criterion follows?

Without these questions, “physics of making” becomes aesthetic language. With them, it remains a research direction whose operational claims can earn or lose force.
