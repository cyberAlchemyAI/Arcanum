## Verification Resolution

Original findings **4, 6, 16, and 22** are dropped, not softened. Finding 4 overreached by turning one useful teaching sequence into universal Craft law; finding 6 proposed metadata relabeling without a learner-visible effect; finding 16 inferred passage the prior artifact did not claim; and finding 22 treated a preferred full replay as a demonstrated defect.

The corrected lower-layer criterion is: local closure is insufficient or irresponsible for the objective; the candidate layer declares its boundary and interfaces; residue decreases; and local validation, upper validation, and recomposition pass. The clean-room story may stage one bounded failed repair because it is a clear beginner witness. That scene is a narrative choice, not a mandatory precondition for every lower-layer trial.
