## Three Essay Theses

### Essay 1: The Table Is Not The Project

Thesis: a familiar object only becomes buildable when hidden requirements are
named, bounded, researched, shaped, built, checked, and reflected on. This must
not collapse into "define clearly before building"; the visible hinge is
schema/data translation at the plan-drawing stage. Cite: **Explorer 1 /
Strongest Arc**, **Explorer 1 / Narrative Moves / Schema**, and **Explorer 3 /
Non-Negotiable Spine**.

Recommended witness scene: plain table reveal becomes a specific bounded table:
a small work desk for a real room, use, material, and stability need. This is a
derived narrative recommendation from **Explorer 1 / Strongest Arc** and
**Explorer 2 / Table Lesson Flow**, not a direct source claim.

### Essay 2: When One Step Becomes Its Own Build

Thesis: a step becomes a craft layer when it needs its own target, facts,
design/schema, plan, tools, validation, residue handling, and recomposition
path. Splitting is justified only when residue decreases and the result can
rejoin the whole. Cite: **Explorer 1 / Narrative Moves / Craft layer**,
**Explorer 2 / Where Cognitive Load Breaks**, **Explorer 3 / Non-Negotiable
Spine**, and **Explorer 3 / Collapse Warnings**.

Recommended witness scene: "make the legs" becomes an ornate-leg subproject with
carving, proportions, tools, practice, fit checks, and reattachment. This is a
derived narrative recommendation from **Explorer 1 / Narrative Moves / Craft
layer** and **Explorer 2 / Where Cognitive Load Breaks**.

### Essay 3: Why Vibe Coding Drifts

Thesis: vague feature names contain too many valid meanings for a probabilistic,
contextual, relational, attentive translator. Craft reduces drift by finding the
smallest responsible schema/data layer that can be built, checked, and
recomposed. Cite: **Explorer 1 / Narrative Moves / Entropy**, **Explorer 2 /
Three Essay Directions**, and **Explorer 3 / Non-Negotiable Spine**.

Recommended witness scene: "make me a dashboard" yields a plausible but
misaligned screen, then user, data, action, success, and validation constrain the
build. This is a derived narrative recommendation from **Explorer 1 / Narrative
Moves / Entropy** and **Explorer 2 / Three Essay Directions**.

## Risks

- Do not frame Craft as better planning. Planning is one phase inside the larger
  cycle. Cite: **Explorer 3 / Collapse Warnings**.
- Do not frame Craft as making things smaller. The unit must remain coherent and
  recomposable. Cite: **Explorer 1 / Risks**, **Explorer 3 / Non-Negotiable
  Spine**, and **Explorer 3 / Collapse Warnings**.
- Do not frame residue as failure, wisdom, or poetic leftover. It is operational
  mismatch to route. Cite: **Explorer 1 / Risks**, **Explorer 2 / Table Lesson
  Flow**, **Explorer 3 / Non-Negotiable Spine**.
- Do not frame entropy as "AI is unreliable." The drift problem is schema/data
  ambiguity amplified by PCRA translation. Cite: **Explorer 1 / Narrative Moves
  / Entropy**, **Explorer 2 / Three Essay Directions**, and **Explorer 3 /
  Non-Negotiable Spine**.
- Do not let layering become endless splitting. A layer must reduce residue and
  recompose meaningfully. Cite: **Explorer 3 / Non-Negotiable Spine** and
  **Explorer 3 / Collapse Warnings**.
