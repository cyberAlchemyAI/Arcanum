# Source And Claim Trace

## Claim Postures

| Posture | Meaning | Video treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source | Directly supported by a named source. | May be stated with a source reference. |
| Synthesis | Integrates multiple source-supported ideas into a local explanation. | State plainly, but do not imply the source used the exact formulation. |
| Analogy | Transfers a useful structure from another domain. | Name the comparison and its limit. |
| Metaphor | Authorial device used to make a relation visible. | Never present as evidence for the method. |

## Prohibited Overclaims

- Craft has been scientifically proven to improve agent performance.
- Category theory proves the operational Craft method.
- Every mismatch requires another layer.
- More decomposition always reduces uncertainty.
- Residue is inherently beneficial.
- CyberAlchemy makes agents autonomous epistemic authorities.
- Browser correctness or a polished render proves that viewers learned.
