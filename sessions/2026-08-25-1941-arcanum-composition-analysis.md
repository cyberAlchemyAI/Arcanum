# Arcanum composition analysis: reviewed research baseline

## Summary

The session set out to test whether the current Arcanum introduction faithfully explains how its capabilities and artifacts relate. Repository inspection exposed Invoke as the missing owner of intent-to-artifact preparation and showed that the relationships among Craft, Task Session, Decision Gate, Invoke, and Goal cannot safely be reduced to a single linear chain. The user's existing introduction was preserved as the reader-facing core of one analysis document. A local research folder and informational baseline were created to separate current contracts, implementation or execution evidence, unresolved relations, and later improvement decisions. An adversarial review found four major defects in that baseline: premature classification of state as transverse, compound evidence obligations, a normative architecture judgment, and no registered question for the meaning of communication and composition. Those defects were corrected by a program question and ten supporting questions with explicit necessity, stable IDs, and distinct evidence boundaries. The initial-definitions validator passed, while the architectural research itself was not executed and no findings about the final composition were claimed. Craft records that unexecuted research as the next governed move before the analysis is expanded or the introduction is revised.

## Open questions

- Which operational definitions of capability communication and composition are supported by current Arcanum authority?
- Which documented relationships are implemented or observed, and who initiates, transports, applies, or records each relation?
- Which relationship gaps materially limit Arcanum's documented purposes, and which separations are deliberate ownership boundaries?

## Next steps

2. Execute the research and persist raw evidence plus findings with per-RQ status, evidence class, contrary evidence, uncertainty, and boundary.
3. Expand `analysis.md` with the supported current-state composition and improvement opportunities, then run a separate review of that completed analysis.

## Recommendation

Resolve the operational meanings and cross-capability relations before claiming an emergent reusable capability; the revised RQ set licenses that order, but the answer remains open until the governed research is executed.
